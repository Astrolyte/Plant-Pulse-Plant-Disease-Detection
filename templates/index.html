Common Questions
About Plant Disease Detection

How accurate is the disease detection model?

Our CNN model achieves over 90% accuracy on well-lit and focused leaf images, but performance may vary based on image quality and plant type.

What kind of data does the model require?

The model requires clear images of plant leaves, preferably taken in natural lighting without blur or shadows.

How do I know if my plant is healthy or diseased?

The model classifies the leaf image and provides a result such as "Healthy" or a specific disease name along with treatment suggestions.

Is the plant detection tool free to use?

Yes, this plant health detection tool is completely free for students, farmers, and gardening enthusiasts.

Can I upload multiple images for detection?

Currently, the system supports one image at a time. Support for bulk image upload is coming soon.

How can I improve detection results?

Ensure the leaf image is in focus, well-lit, and captured against a plain background for the best model accuracy.
